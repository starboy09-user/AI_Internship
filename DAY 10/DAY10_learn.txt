DAY 10 – AI Image Generation

What I Learned:

1. AI Image Generation
- AI can create images from text prompts.
- Tools like DALL-E, Midjourney, and Stable Diffusion generate images.
- Better prompts create better images.

2. Poster Creation
- Posters can be generated for events, promotions, and advertisements.
- Important elements:
  * Title
  * Theme
  * Colors
  * Background
  * Call to Action

3. Festival Creatives
- AI can create festival greetings and promotional banners.
- Examples:
  * Diwali Poster
  * Holi Greeting
  * Christmas Banner
  * New Year Creative

4. Business Creatives
- AI helps create:
  * Social Media Ads
  * Product Posters
  * Sale Banners
  * Brand Promotions

5. Prompt Writing for Images
- A prompt describes what image should be generated.
- Good prompts include:
  * Subject
  * Style
  * Colors
  * Lighting
  * Background

Example Prompts:

1. Festival Poster:
"Beautiful Diwali celebration poster with glowing diyas, fireworks, golden lighting, modern design, high quality."

2. Business Advertisement:
"Professional smartphone advertisement poster with modern background, product spotlight, premium design, 4K."

3. Social Media Creative:
"Instagram promotional banner for clothing brand, stylish models, vibrant colors, professional marketing design."

Key Takeaways:
- Detailed prompts produce better images.
- AI saves time in graphic design.
- AI-generated posters are useful for marketing and social media.
