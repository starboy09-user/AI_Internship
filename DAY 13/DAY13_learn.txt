DAY 13 – Voice AI Assistant

Topics Learned:

1. Speech Recognition Basics
- Speech recognition converts spoken words into text.
- Python can use microphones to capture voice input.
- The SpeechRecognition library helps recognize speech.

2. Voice Assistant
- A voice assistant listens to user commands.
- It converts speech to text.
- It processes commands and performs actions.

3. Execute Voice Commands
Examples:
- Open Google
- Tell current time
- Say hello
- Exit application

Libraries Used:
- SpeechRecognition
- PyAudio
- webbrowser
- datetime

Applications:
- AI Assistants
- Smart Devices
- Virtual Automation
- Voice Controlled Systems

Outcome:
Built a mini voice assistant that listens to voice commands and performs tasks automatically.
